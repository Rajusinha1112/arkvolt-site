Our Partners

Building Partnerships for Innovation and Sustainable Growth

At ARKVOLT, meaningful partnerships form the foundation of our engineering, research, and entrepreneurship initiatives. By collaborating with academic institutions, industry experts, technology organizations, and government bodies, we contribute to a collaborative ecosystem supporting clean energy, applied technology, skill development, and entrepreneurship across India.

Our partnerships, together with T-0's technology and engineering network, help bridge academia, industry, and innovation, creating opportunities for research, technology transfer, workforce development, and enterprise growth.
