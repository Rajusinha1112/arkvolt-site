ARKVOLT Incubation Centre

Innovation Beyond Energy

The ARKVOLT Entrepreneurship & Innovation Incubation Centre is an initiative established to support entrepreneurs, innovators, startups, and young professionals in developing ideas into sustainable enterprises.

The centre provides a collaborative ecosystem offering structured guidance, industry mentorship, business development support, and access to academic and institutional partnerships. Working alongside T-0, the centre also supports founders building in energy, software, AI, hardware, and deep tech sectors.

What We Offer

  • Entrepreneurship Development Programmes
  • Business Idea Validation
  • Startup Incubation
  • Business Planning & Strategy
  • Industry Mentorship
  • Startup Registration Guidance
  • Government & Institutional Support Guidance
  • Networking Opportunities
  • Enterprise Development Support
  • Technology and Product Incubation

Dream Come True Programme

The Dream Come True Programme is a structured 60-Day Entrepreneurship & Startup Development Programme designed to provide aspiring entrepreneurs with practical business knowledge, mentorship, and incubation support.

Participants receive guidance throughout their entrepreneurial journey, from developing an idea to launching and growing an enterprise.

Programme Eligibility

Age

18–40 Years

Educational Qualification

  • 12th Pass or Equivalent
  • Graduate (Any Discipline)
  • Diploma Holders
  • Working Professionals

  3. Training & Mentorship
